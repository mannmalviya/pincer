## Copy rules

- **Never use em dashes (`—`) anywhere in dashboard UI text, microcopy, comments,
  or any other writing surface in this directory.** Em dashes look polished in
  isolation but they're a recognizable AI-writing tell. Use a period, a comma,
  a semicolon, or parentheses instead. If the impulse to write " — " shows up,
  rewrite the sentence; don't just swap punctuation.
